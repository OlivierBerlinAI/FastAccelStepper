FastAccelStepper.js Demo - Dual Steppers

Demo Setup:
• Stepper 1: Normal direction (dirHighCountsUp = true)
• Stepper 2: Inverted direction (dirHighCountsUp = false)

How it works:
• Both steppers use the same position coordinate system (0, 1000, 2000, etc.)
• The visualization shows stepper 2 moving in the opposite physical direction
• Try "Move 1000" on both - they both reach position 1000, but move opposite ways!
• Perfect for polargraphs where motors are physically mirrored
